My love,

I don't think you'll ever truly understand how much you mean to me, no matter how many times I try to put it into words. Sometimes I have so many things I want to tell you, so many feelings I want you to understand, but words never seem enough to explain what's in my heart. You've become such a big part of my life without me even realizing when it happened. Somewhere along the way, you became the person I want to share all my little moments with, the first person I think of when something good happens, and the one I wish I could talk to whenever life gets hard. Even on the most ordinary days, just having you in my life makes everything feel a little more special.

I hope you know that you never have to be perfect for me to love you. Even on the days when you feel insecure, when you doubt yourself, or when you think you're not doing enough, I hope you remember that you're more than enough for me. I love the way you think, the way you speak, your kindness, your little habits — especially your knuckle cracking and the way you comfort me — and all the things that make you uniquely you. I love the sides of you that you show everyone and the softer parts that you only let a few people see. You don't have to hide your bad days or pretend to be okay around me. I want to be there for you when you're happy, but I also want to be there when things aren't going your way.

I care about the little things more than you might realize. I wonder if you've eaten, if you've had enough rest, if something is on your mind, or if you're keeping something to yourself because you don't want to bother anyone. I want to know how your day went, what made you smile, what upset you, and even the random things that might not seem important to anyone else. It's not something I force myself to do — it's just what happens when someone means this much to you. Your feelings matter to me, your happiness matters to me, and even when I can't fix whatever you're going through, I still want you to know that you don't have to face everything alone.

When I look back at the moments we've shared, I realize how many little memories have become so precious to me. The conversations, the laughter, the random talks, the times you've made me smile without even trying, and all those moments that might seem small but mean so much to me. You've brought a kind of happiness into my life that I never want to take for granted. You've made me realize that loving someone isn't only about the big romantic moments, but it's also about checking on them, listening to them, remembering the smallest details, and choosing to care about them even on the most ordinary days.

So whenever you have a bad day, whenever you feel like you're difficult to love, or whenever you start questioning your place in my life, please remember this: you mean so much more to me than I could ever explain. You don't need to earn my care by being happy all the time, and you don't need to have everything figured out to deserve love. I cherish you for who you are, not for who you think you need to become. I want to see you grow, achieve everything you dream of, and become the happiest version of yourself, and I hope I can be someone who makes that journey a little more beautiful. I love you more than these words could ever show, and if there's one thing I hope you never doubt, it's that you are deeply loved, genuinely valued, and so incredibly special to me.
